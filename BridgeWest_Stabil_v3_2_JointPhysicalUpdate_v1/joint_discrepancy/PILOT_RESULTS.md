# Joint-discrepancy pilot results

## Frozen development information

- 2024 force-normalised shaker amplitudes set the bending/torsion modal-residue scales to 1.5986838972 and 0.9464728241.
- 2022 static bogie weighing and permitted reference strains A031/B053 set a single strain-recovery scale. Across the 12 2022 passages the event-wise scale ranges from 0.30376 to 0.48222; the frozen median is 0.4322035297.
- No 2025 static weighing, target strain or PLS displacement is used before the 2025 predictions are frozen.

## Interpretation

The experiment supports the hypothesis that the earlier failure was not simply a rail-contact geometry problem. Frequency/damping discrepancy alone leaves two amplitude directions unconstrained: (i) absolute force-to-displacement compliance and (ii) strain recovery relative to load. Independent shaker amplitude information and an independently weighed development campaign constrain those two directions without using 2025 target strain or PLS data. The remaining passage-to-passage load error indicates that individual bogie identification and load-transfer modelling are still imperfect.
