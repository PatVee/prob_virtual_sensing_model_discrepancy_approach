# Bridge West: Stabil + shaker-informed discrepancy v3.1

## Implemented production question

Can the two measured structural reference strains **A031 and B053**, together with the frozen detailed Stabil model and its shaker-informed discrepancy ensemble, predict the withheld strains **A058, A070, B031 and B081** over **12 passages from 2022 and 24 passages from 2025**?

The production implementation under `production/` now enforces that question literally. During each passage inference, only A031/B053 structural histories are read. The four target measurements are first opened by the separate assessment stage after prediction.

The detailed model uses the Garather Bach-specific top-flange restraint documented by Lorenzen; Table 5.2 from the HUMVIB footbridge is not imported. Structural uncertainty is represented by the frozen 84 weighted shaker states derived from the ST-W campaign. The old arbitrary structural perturbation grid is inactive.

No second target-derived discrepancy calibration is part of v3.1.

## Added displacement output

The same passage posterior is also propagated to the 41-point vertical-displacement fields of girders BA and BB. Displacement is therefore predicted for every successfully inferred passage without using PLS measurements during inference. For 2025 passages with a PLS record, a separate assessment-only stage compares the predicted and measured own-midspan-peak spatial profiles. The plotting stage produces a BA/BB event figure and a 2025 campaign summary of peak-amplitude transfer and spatial NRMSE.
